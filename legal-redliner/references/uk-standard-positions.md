# UK MSA — Standard Policy Positions

Internal standard positions for Master Service Agreements governed by English law. Loaded on demand when redlining UK MSA clauses.

## Definitions (Clause Type: Definitions)

**Position:** Definitions should be comprehensive and unambiguous. Ensure:
- "Confidential Information" includes a broad but bounded definition with clear exclusions
- "Deliverables" and "Services" are clearly separated
- All key commercial terms are defined, not left implicit
- Defined terms use consistent capitalisation throughout

## Scope of Services (Clause Type: Scope)

**Position:** Scope should be governed by SOWs, with clear change control:
- SOWs should be the primary mechanism for defining scope
- Change control procedure should be referenced or included
- Professional standards clause should be mutual where possible
- No open-ended obligations ("best efforts" preferred over "shall ensure")

## Fees and Payment (Clause Type: Commercial)

**Position:** Payment terms should be balanced:
- Net 30 is acceptable; Net 60+ requires commercial justification
- Late payment interest should reference the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 statutory rate (8% + Bank of England base rate), not a custom lower rate
- Invoicing in arrears is standard; advance payment requires security
- Right to dispute invoices within a reasonable period (30 days)

## Intellectual Property (Clause Type: IP)

**Position:** Customer should own deliverables:
- All bespoke deliverables created for Customer should vest in Customer on creation
- Supplier retains pre-existing IP with a broad licence to Customer
- Customer grants no IP to Supplier beyond what's needed for service delivery
- Work product assignment clause should be explicit, not implied
- Moral rights waiver should be included

**Red flag:** Supplier ownership of deliverables is NON-STANDARD — redline to customer ownership.

## Confidentiality (Clause Type: Confidentiality)

**Position:** Standard mutual confidentiality with:
- Obligations surviving termination (minimum 3 years, ideally 5 years for trade secrets)
- Standard carve-outs (public domain, prior knowledge, independent development, legal compulsion)
- Permitted disclosure to professional advisers and affiliates under equivalent obligations
- Return or destruction obligation on termination

## Data Protection (Clause Type: Data Processing)

**Position:** Must comply with UK GDPR and DPA 2018:
- Clear controller/processor roles
- Processing only on documented instructions
- Sub-processor controls: prior specific or general written authorisation with objection right
- Data breach notification within 72 hours (or "without undue delay" matching UK GDPR Art 33)
- International transfer controls (UK IDTA or UK Addendum to EU SCCs)
- Data subject rights assistance obligations
- Audit rights for Customer
- Data deletion/return on termination
- Separate DPA recommended for complex processing

**Red flag:** Missing sub-processor controls or breach notification timeline.

## Liability (Clause Type: Liability)

**Position:** Liability should be mutual and balanced:
- Mutual cap at 100-150% of annual fees (12-month rolling)
- Carve-outs from cap: death/personal injury, fraud, IP infringement, data breach, confidentiality breach
- Indirect/consequential loss exclusion is acceptable if mutual
- Nothing should limit liability for matters that cannot be limited by law (s.2(1) UCTA 1977: negligence causing death/personal injury)

**Red flag:** One-sided liability cap favouring Supplier only. Cap below 100% of annual fees.

## Term and Termination (Clause Type: Termination)

**Position:** Balanced termination rights:
- Termination for convenience with 30-60 days notice (90 days is too long for Customer)
- Termination for material breach with reasonable cure period (30 days)
- Termination for insolvency
- Auto-renewal acceptable but notice period should be 30 days, not 90
- Transition assistance obligation on termination (minimum 90 days at prevailing rates)
- Data return/deletion within 30 days of termination

**Red flag:** Auto-renewal with 90-day notice period. No termination for convenience.

## Governing Law (Clause Type: Governing Law)

**Position:** English law with English courts:
- English law governing law is preferred
- Exclusive jurisdiction of English courts
- Consider mediation/arbitration as a first step for disputes
- No foreign arbitration requirements
- CEDR or similar UK-based mediation is acceptable

## General Provisions (Clause Type: Boilerplate)

**Position:**
- Entire agreement clause should be included
- Amendments in writing and signed by both parties
- Assignment requires consent (not to be unreasonably withheld)
- Force majeure should be included with notification obligations
- Severability clause
- Waiver clause (failure to exercise right doesn't waive it)
- Third party rights: exclude Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999
