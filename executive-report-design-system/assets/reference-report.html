Zava Q2 Executive Summary · Method

Method and assumptions

How every figure in this report was derived

Read this before quoting a number outside the executive team

Revenue recognition

Closed revenue is the total contract value of deals whose stage moved to Closed Won with a close date inside the quarter. It is booking value, not recognised revenue, and it is not adjusted for ramp or phasing. Where a contract spans more than one year, the full committed value is counted in the quarter it was signed.

Renewals are excluded throughout unless a section says otherwise. Two multi-region agreements are attributed to the signing entity rather than the account headquarters, which is why the regional split does not reconcile to the account list.

Pipeline

Open pipeline counts every deal not in Closed Won or Closed Lost at the close of the quarter, at full value and unweighted. We deliberately do not publish a probability-weighted figure at this level: the weighting model was last recalibrated in 2024 and currently overstates late-stage conversion by roughly six points.

Delivery health

Project health is the share of active initiatives marked On Track by their delivery lead at the last weekly review. It is self-reported. It is useful as a trend and unreliable as an absolute.

Average velocity is the mean change in completion percentage across active initiatives over the trailing seven days. Completed initiatives are excluded, because a finished project would otherwise drag the mean towards zero and mask real movement.

Known limitations

  • Figures are taken at 30 June and are not restated for later corrections.
  • One initiative changed owner mid-quarter; its early status history is incomplete.
  • Currency is reported as booked, with no retranslation at quarter-end rates.
Caveat

Delivery health is self-reported

Both at-risk initiatives were marked on track in the preceding two reviews, so treat the headline as a lagging indicator.

Source: CRM export 30 June 2026; delivery review minutes to 24 June 2026. Prepared by Revenue Operations.

The architecture

Three layers — only one changes per report

1

Style

CSS custom properties define every visual decision — colour, type, spacing, radius. The same four palette classes apply to all reports identically.

palette-warm-paper palette-deep-ink palette-clean-white palette-slate

Deterministic — same class, same output, always.

2

Components

Each skill has a catalogue of named blocks — hero sentence, KPI row, trend chart, bar list, compare strip, highlights. The AI reads the data and decides which blocks to use and in what order.

AI-assembled — the skill picks the right blocks for the story.

3

Data

Before rendering, the skill builds a validated JSON document — the “consistency layer”. Every block, every value and every label is in the JSON.

Same JSON → same HTML. Every time. No drift.
